Key words

flow rate
How much liquid passes a point in a given time, e.g. 200 cm³ per minute.
constant flow
The liquid flows in at the same rate the whole time.
cross-section
The shape you see when you slice straight across a solid.
prism
A 3-D shape with the same cross-section all the way along its length.

Watch out!

  • Each picture is the front of a prism. Every container is the same thickness from front to back.
  • The flow rate stays constant, even when the graph is curved. What changes is how fast the depth rises.
  • Vertical sides give a straight line. Sloping or curved sides give a curve.
  • The wider the container, the more slowly the depth rises (a less steep graph).
  • A sudden change in width gives a sudden change in gradient (a corner).
  • Containers of the same height can take very different times to fill.
