Move the slider and the sample grows or shrinks in front of you. New sample takes a fresh one of the same size from the same population. Quartiles by the A-level discrete-data method: work out ¼n, ½n and ¾n, take the midpoint of that value and the next when the position is a whole number, and round up when it is not. Standard deviation uses the divisor n. The population is Normal, with a mean between 40 and 60 and a standard deviation between 7 and 15, held inside the 0–100 scale. It is symmetric, so its true mean and true median are the same value to within a tenth.
